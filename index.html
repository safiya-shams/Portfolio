PROJECT 02Machine Learning

Smart Accident Risk Classification Using Traffic & Signal Data

Developed an end-to-end classification workflow to predict accident occurrence from traffic-signal, weather, road, and location features. Cleaned and validated the data, performed EDA, and applied encoding, scaling, and feature engineering to produce a dataset of 1,102 rows and 33 columns.
